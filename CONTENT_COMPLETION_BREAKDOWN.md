## The real gaps (what "missing" means)

### 1. Module videos — the headline gap
Every module embeds a famous third-party YouTube video (e.g. 3Blue1Brown) as an explicit placeholder. Modules 02–10 carry a visible disclaimer: _"the embed above is a placeholder intro video."_ No self-hosted videos exist (zero `.mp4`/`.webm`). Remotion JSON scripts (≈3.5–4.2 KB) exist for all 10 modules in `public/videos/scripts/`, ready to render — but nothing has been rendered.

### 2. Transcripts — scaffold only
All 10 `.vtt` files in `public/videos/transcripts/` are hand-written scaffolds (17–26 cues each) explicitly marked _"Scaffold transcript… Replace with Whisper output once the recorded lecture is available."_

### 3. Captions — scaffold only
All 10 `.vtt` files in `public/videos/captions/` are short placeholder stubs (~37 lines) flagged for replacement once real videos are recorded.

## Not real gaps (intentional or cosmetic)

- **`lab-01-…-practical-labs.mdx` `# TODO` stubs (modules 2–10):** intentional student exercises (anchor generators, training loops, mAP eval), not unfinished content.
- **Empty `content/resources/` folder:** the live `/resources` page works from a hardcoded link array in `src/app/resources/page.tsx`; the folder is just a dangling, unused directory.
- **Both `theory.md` and `theory.mdx` per module:** `.md` is the source, `.mdx` is generated by `scripts/convert-theory.mjs`. Expected, not duplication to fix.
